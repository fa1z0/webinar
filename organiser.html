About the Organisers

Host Institution

AIMST University — Faculty of Dentistry

AIMST University, Malaysia is a leading private university known for its strong research culture and academic excellence across health sciences. Its Faculty of Dentistry is committed to producing socially responsible, clinically competent dental professionals and is proud to host the "Graduation to Global Practice" online workshop. Designed to empower dental graduates seeking international careers, this initiative provides vital guidance on licensing, registration pathways, and professional requirements for practicing in destination countries such as the UK, New Zealand, and the USA.

Virtual Clinical Training (VCT)

VCT is a Malaysian online continuing education platform tailored for dental students and practitioners. It offers interactive virtual sessions, case discussions, and specialized dental webinars — including exam mastery coaching for the MFDS and clinical workshops — to bridge theoretical knowledge and practical patient care.

Dental Diary Malaysia

Dental Diary Malaysia (operating as Dental Diary Academy) is a continuous professional development and training provider for dental practitioners in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Founded by Dr. Vinesh Raj, the academy offers hands-on clinical courses, exam coaching (such as MFDS/MFD), and specialized training workshops.
