About the Workshop

“Graduation to Global Practice: Your Dental Career Journey” is an online webinar featuring distinguished speakers to support dental students, recent graduates, Dental Officers, and early-career practitioners as they prepare to move from university-based training into professional practice and, eventually, towards wider national or international career opportunities.

The online webinar will feature:
  • Invited international and national speakers from diverse backgrounds
  • Interactive Q&A sessions to encourage knowledge sharing and guide participants

The webinar is designed to be a forward-looking platform for preparing dental graduates for Dental Practice in Malaysia and international career pathways & entrepreneurship.

Background and Rationale

Transition from graduate to practitioner matters for career mobility and patient safety. Graduates may follow diverse career pathways: government service, private practice, academia/research, hospital dentistry, public health, military, corporate healthcare, or overseas practice. Each pathway has different requirements: registration systems, licensing exams, language proficiency, professional standards, immigration rules, and scope-of-practice expectations.

  • Professional registration with the Malaysian Dental Council (MDC).
  • Annual Practicing Certificate (APC) renewal requirements and timelines.
  • Completion of required Continuing Professional Development (CPD) hours.
  • Adherence to MDC ethical codes and professional practice standards.
  • Compliance with laws and regulations (e.g., Health Services Act, infection control guidelines).
  • Building competency in professional conduct, regulatory awareness, and documentation improves readiness for local practice and international mobility.
  • Early planning: map desired career pathways against specific regulatory, exam, language, and immigration requirements to reduce barriers later.
  • Practical tips: keep CPD records, maintain indemnity cover, understand APC deadlines, and seek mentorship on specialty or overseas registration pathways.

Workshop Objectives

1
Understand the knowledge and skills required to begin dental practice confidently and responsibly in Malaysia.
2
Recognise professional, ethical, legal, and regulatory requirements governing practice, including MDC registration, APC, CPD hours, patient safety, and scope of practice.
3
Gain an overview of specialty postings, including Dental Officer responsibilities, case management, teamwork, emergency care, consultation, and referral pathways.
4
Understand key considerations in establishing and managing a practice: planning, financing, regulatory compliance, staffing, infection control, and quality assurance.
5
Gain introductory knowledge of international dental registration pathways, qualification assessments, language requirements, licensing exams, and supervised practice.
6
Receive practical guidance, realistic expectations, and inspiration to transition smoothly from graduate to a confident, ethical, and globally prepared professional.

Scope, Key Themes & Structure

The workshop covers a wide range of essential topics organized systematically under three core pillars.

Pillar Focus Areas
Preparing for Dental Practice in Malaysia
Focus on the knowledge and skills required to begin practice confidently and responsibly within the Malaysian healthcare system. This session is particularly important for graduates because professional readiness involves much more than completing a dental degree.
Career Development, Entrepreneurship & Future Practice
  • Addresses the transition from clinician to practice owner or dental entrepreneur.
  • Helps participants understand the opportunities, expectations, working environments, career progression, service commitments, and professional development available within government and military dental services.
Overseas Registration Examination
Focuses on career opportunities beyond Malaysia and the expanding roles of dentists as innovators, practice owners, educators, researchers, and global healthcare leaders.

Expected Outcomes

  • Explain the official regulatory and licensing pathways for practicing dentistry within Malaysia.
